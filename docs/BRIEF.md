# Space Snack Quest — the opening

This is the first small build. It is only the start of Chapter 1.

## What you do

1. The title is on screen and Joe's song plays. The song here is a new, simple tune. Joe's own recording is not in this build.
2. Mops talks to Squatch in the morning. Tap each line.
3. SING lights up. Tap it and they sing the goodbye. Mops leaves.
4. A card says "That evening…"
5. Squatch is in the hall. You walk him to the plant. The plant's own shape glows. Nothing walks him there for you.
6. HIDE lights up when he is close. Tap it. His eyes sit low in the front leaves.
7. If a guest sees him, he hops, a lockup card shows, and that walk starts again at the doorway.

## How it should feel

- The next thing to tap glows.
- People who can see have a dashed cone. It fills in when Squatch is inside it.
- After Mops leaves, the evening sneak begins.
- Being seen restarts that sneak, not the whole story.
- This is its own game. Sneaky Sasquatch is only the idea of a small sneak.

## Rules for the pictures and the words

- The rooms are Brian's locked pictures. They are not redrawn.
- Mops's beard is his mop. There is no mop standing in the room.
- Lines on screen are the ones already approved. A line this build needs and does not have is shown as "[line needed]" and listed for Brian.
- Bubbles sit on the person who is talking and stay off faces.
- Story lines wait for a tap. Walking to sing, Mops leaving, and the hop play on their own.

## Approved lines used

- Stay in here.
