# Game Brief: Space Snack Quest

## In your words

Build the first small playable build of Space Snack Quest v2. Opening beats of Chapter 1 only, in order:

1. Title screen and Joe's song.
2. Mops's morning lines.
3. A SING tap for the goodbye song.
4. A "That evening..." card.
5. Squatch sneaks to the plant.
6. HIDE.
7. A jail card if Squatch is seen, then restart.

The six design picks Brian approved:

- The next thing to tap is lit up.
- Everyone has a dashed sight cone.
- Simple original music, with no copyrighted melodies.
- After Mops leaves, the evening sneak begins.
- Being spotted shows the jail card and restarts the beat.
- It's its own game, with Sneaky Sasquatch only as inspiration.

Hard rules he set for this build: use only the locked room pictures; Mops's beard is his mop and there is no separate mop prop; no on-screen words beyond approved lines (a missing line is a placeholder, listed for him); dialogue never mentions things that aren't in the current room; every button does something, and chips never offer actions the player can't do yet; speech bubbles sit on the right character and never cover faces; story pauses wait for a tap, except clear cutscenes; stage directions become animation or sound, not words; goal hints outline the real destination's shape (the planter), never a circle or box; the player sneaks there, with no auto-walk; when spotted, Squatch makes a quick visible hop, not a teleport; hidden Squatch's eyes sit low in the plant's front leaves; taps only, on a phone-sized and iPad-sized screen.

**Pitch:** A furry kid sneaks out after bedtime to hide in a plant, and if someone sees him he hops, the lockup card shows, and that sneak starts again.

**Genre:** A tap-to-sneak story. *(inferred (strong))*

**Controls:** Taps only. Tap the lit thing to go on. Tap the floor to walk Squatch. Tap SING, then HIDE.

**Question:** Can a kid follow the opening — the title, the morning, the goodbye song, the sneak to the plant, and a catch that starts that sneak again — with taps only?

**Hooks:** The lit next tap, the dashed sight cones, and hiding in the real plant.

**Intent:** staged failure *(you said)* · outside the window *(you said: phone and iPad)*

## Look and references
- Look: the locked room pictures stay as they are, flat and outlined, with Squatch a brown furry kid and Mops a white goat whose beard is his mop. Dashed sight cones. The planter's own shape glows. Every figure drawn, no emoji, no plain circles.
- References: Sneaky Sasquatch is inspiration only (a small character sneaking past cones). This game keeps its own rooms, people, song and story.
- View: 2D · Camera: one locked room, still, filling the screen. *(you said: the locked pictures)*

## First minute
The title glows. A tap starts Joe's song, an original tune, and the morning. A card says who Squatch is. Mops says good morning, mentions the party, and tells him to stay. Each line waits for a tap. SING lights up. They sing the goodbye, Mops leaves, and a card says that evening. Squatch is in the hall doorway. The planter's shape glows. He walks there himself.

## Challenge
On the way to the plant a guest looks across the floor, then looks away, on a steady loop. Step in the dashed cone while he is looking and Squatch hops, the lockup card shows, and the sneak starts again at the doorway. Hiding in the plant, eyes low in the front leaves, is the way through. Later chapters are not in this build.

## Core loop
- Tap the lit thing, and the morning moves on.
- Walk Squatch around the guest's cone to the glowing planter.
- Tap HIDE, and he is in the plant. Get seen, and that walk starts again.

## Goal & fail state
- Hide in the plant. Being seen shows the lockup card and restarts the sneak at the doorway.
